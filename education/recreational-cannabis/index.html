Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey: The Basics

By Greenstop Wellness4 min readReviewed November 2026

New to legal cannabis in New Jersey? This guide covers who can buy, how much, where you can use it and other rules worth knowing, with every legal point linked to its official source.

Who can buy

New Jersey's Cannabis Regulatory, Enforcement Assistance, and Marketplace Modernization (CREAMM) Act allows the legal sale and use of cannabis and cannabis products for residents 21 and older, and anyone 21 or older, New Jersey resident or visitor, can buy recreational cannabis at a licensed dispensary.

From official New Jersey sources

  • New Jersey's CREAMM Act allows the legal sale and use of cannabis and cannabis products for residents 21 years and older. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Anyone 21 or older, whether a New Jersey resident or a visitor, can buy cannabis. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Adults 21 and older can buy recreational cannabis at a licensed dispensary. General Information: Legal Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)

Bring a government-issued ID. Staff must see it to confirm you are 21 or older, and may not copy it or keep a record of your purchase beyond what that single transaction requires. Under the state rules, they check photo ID before you enter and again before each sale. Buying anywhere other than a New Jersey licensed dispensary is illegal, and the safety of that product cannot be guaranteed.

From official New Jersey sources

  • Dispensary staff must see a government-issued ID to confirm you are 21 or older, and they may not copy your ID or keep a record of your purchase beyond what that single transaction requires. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Under the state rules, a retailer checks photo ID before letting a customer in and again before each sale, and may not keep a copy of the ID. N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Buying cannabis anywhere other than a New Jersey licensed dispensary is illegal, and the safety of that product cannot be guaranteed. General Information: Legal Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)

How much you can buy

In each transaction, a dispensary may sell you up to the equivalent of 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of usable cannabis. According to the New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission, that means up to:

  • 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of dried flower, or
  • 4 grams of solid concentrate or resin, or the equivalent of 4 grams of concentrate in liquid form, or
  • 4 grams of vaporized formulations (oil), a cap the state rules express as vaporized formulations containing no more than four mL of oil, or
  • 1,000 mg of ingestible products such as gummies, for example ten 100 mg packages.

From official New Jersey sources

  • In each transaction, a dispensary may sell you up to the equivalent of 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of usable cannabis. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • In one transaction you can buy up to 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of dried flower. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • In one transaction you can buy up to 4 grams of solid cannabis concentrate or resin, or the equivalent of 4 grams of concentrate in liquid form. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • In one transaction you can buy up to 4 grams of vaporized formulations (oil). Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • The rules also cap a single sale at four grams of solid concentrate or four mL of liquid concentrate (oil), and at vaporized formulations containing no more than four mL of oil. N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • In one transaction you can buy up to 1,000 mg of ingestible cannabis products such as gummies, for example ten 100 mg packages. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)

You can combine products, for example 14.17 grams (1/2 ounce) of dried flower and 2 grams of concentrate, as long as the total does not exceed 28.35 grams (one ounce) or the equivalent. Dispensaries must make a good-faith effort to stop customers from going over one ounce across multiple transactions.

From official New Jersey sources

  • You can combine products in one purchase, for example 14.17 grams (1/2 ounce) of dried flower and 2 grams of concentrate, or 5 packages of gummies and 14.17 grams (1/2 ounce) of dried flower. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • A combination purchase may not exceed 28.35 grams (one ounce) or the equivalent, and retailers must make a good-faith effort to stop customers from going over one ounce across multiple transactions. N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens the official source in a new tab)

How much you can have and share

Official texts give different possession figures. The Commission's recreational cannabis page and general FAQ say state law allows adults 21 and older to have up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products in their possession. The state's personal use rules say it is not unlawful for a consumer to possess, purchase, transport or give away without payment up to 1 ounce of usable cannabis, or the equivalent in cannabis products, obtained directly from a licensed retailer. Check the current guidance before relying on either.

From official New Jersey sources

  • New Jersey state law allows adults 21 and older to have up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products in their possession. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • New Jersey state law allows adults 21 and older to have up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products in their possession. General Information: Legal Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • The state rules say it is not unlawful for a consumer to possess, purchase, transport or give away without payment up to 1 ounce of usable cannabis, or the equivalent in cannabis products, obtained directly from a licensed retailer. N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens the official source in a new tab)

You may give up to 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of cannabis to another adult 21 or older, but you may not accept any payment or compensation for it.

From official New Jersey sources

  • You may give up to 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of cannabis to another adult 21 or older, but you may not accept any payment or compensation for it. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)

Where you can use it

You may smoke cannabis in private spaces, and cannabis smoking is allowed where cigarette smoking is permitted, unless stated otherwise. Generally, adults 21 and older can use cannabis on private property, but property owners can limit its use, and landlords may prohibit it on their property. New Jersey's Smoke Free Air Act prohibits smoking of any kind in most public spaces.

From official New Jersey sources

  • You may smoke cannabis in private spaces, but landlords may prohibit cannabis use on their property. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Cannabis smoking is allowed where cigarette smoking is permitted, unless stated otherwise. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Generally, adults 21 and older can use cannabis on private property, but property owners can limit its use on their property. General Information: Legal Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • New Jersey's Smoke Free Air Act prohibits smoking of any kind in most public spaces, and private property owners have the right to prohibit cannabis smoking on their property. Patient FAQs (opens the official source in a new tab)

You can also consume legal cannabis at licensed dispensaries with consumption areas, which admit only patrons 21 or older with photo ID. Consumption areas cannot sell food or drinks. You may bring or order in food and drinks, but no alcohol, and no tobacco products are allowed inside. You may not leave a consumption area with an open package of cannabis.

From official New Jersey sources

  • You can also consume legal cannabis at licensed dispensaries with consumption areas, which admit only patrons 21 or older with photo ID. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Consumption areas cannot sell food or drinks; patrons may bring or order in food and drinks, but no alcohol, and no tobacco products are allowed inside. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Like open-container laws for alcohol, you may not leave a consumption area with an open package of cannabis. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)

Driving, travel and growing at home

Driving under the influence of cannabis is illegal in New Jersey, and an impaired driver can be charged with DUI. Transporting cannabis across state lines is still illegal, so you cannot take cannabis bought here home to another state. Growing cannabis at home is not legal either: current state law does not let the Commission authorize any growing outside a business with a cultivation license.

From official New Jersey sources

  • Driving under the influence of cannabis is illegal in New Jersey, even though cannabis itself is legal for adults 21 and older. Don't Drive High (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • This applies whether cannabis is used for recreational or medical purposes, and an impaired driver can be charged with driving under the influence (DUI). Don't Drive High (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • You cannot take cannabis bought in New Jersey home to another state; transporting cannabis across state lines is still illegal. Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)
  • Growing cannabis at home is not legal; current New Jersey law does not let the Commission authorize any growing outside a business with a cultivation license. General Information: Legal Cannabis in New Jersey (opens the official source in a new tab)

Federal law, the Controlled Substances Act, still prohibits the cultivation, distribution and possession of marijuana regardless of state law.

From official New Jersey sources

  • Federal law, the Controlled Substances Act, still prohibits the cultivation, distribution and possession of marijuana regardless of state law. Cannabis Related Laws (opens the official source in a new tab)

Rules change

For the current rules, check the Commission's recreational cannabis page and general FAQ. For safety tips, see Safe and Responsible Cannabis Use. Questions? Ask the Greenstop team.

Common questions

Do I have to be a New Jersey resident to buy?
No. Anyone 21 or older, resident or visitor, can buy recreational cannabis at a licensed dispensary. Bring a government-issued ID.
How much can I buy in one transaction?
Up to the equivalent of 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of usable cannabis, for example 28.35 grams of dried flower or 1,000 mg of ingestible products.
Can I take cannabis home to another state?
No. Transporting cannabis across state lines is still illegal.
