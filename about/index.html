About Greenstop

Rooted in Wellness. Powered by Community.

At Greenstop Wellness, cannabis isn’t just a product, it’s a way to heal, unwind, and grow. We’re here to guide your journey with empathy, knowledge, and purpose.

Explore ProductsOur Story

Green Stop, Jersey City’s newest premier recreational dispensary, is situated in the heart of Hudson County on Tonnelle Ave, a mere 10-minute drive from the Meadowlands. Greenstop is dedicated to serving and positively impacting the Jersey City community and its residents. We prioritize customer satisfaction and provide high-quality cannabis in a welcoming environment, offering empathy, education, and an unparalleled experience. Our knowledgeable staff ensures a curated selection of products to meet every demand or need, with one of the state’s largest menus catering to all preferences. Drop by today, order online for pickup, or visit us in person for personalized assistance from our friendly budtenders. tep into a world where relaxation and rejuvenation await, as our knowledgeable staff guide you through a personalized journey of exploration and wellness. Embrace a new paradigm of cannabis consumption at Green stop dispensary, where every visit promises an elevated experience beyond expectation.
